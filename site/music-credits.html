Tonal Island

Music credits

The demo uses excerpts from existing works. Every selected edition is marked Public Domain by the Mutopia Project.

These are monophonic adaptations of the published MIDI scores, with accompaniment removed, slightly shorter notes, and some complete excerpts transposed by octaves. The retro sound is synthesized in your browser; no third-party audio recordings are used.
